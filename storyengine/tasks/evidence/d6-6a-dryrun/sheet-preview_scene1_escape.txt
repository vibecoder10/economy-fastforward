This is storyboard sheet 1 of 1: a grid of 6 panels arranged in 3 columns on a plain light grey page. Each panel is a wide 16:9 cinematic frame, never square or tall. Every panel uses the same art style, Photorealistic, cinematic film still, with matching lighting and color grading throughout. Draw every character consistently across every panel, exactly as stated in the CHARACTER block above (when one is present) — never inventing or varying their look. A small plain strip below each panel shows ONLY its panel number and shot type — NOTHING else ever appears in that strip, no matter how a panel's own brief is worded below. The panel artwork carries no lettering, signs, speech bubbles, captions, dialogue or written words of any kind — keep every panel free of readable text except the small panel-number label.
CHARACTER — stated once, drawn identically in every panel: Nyla (a young woman with shoulder-length black shaggy hair…)

POD SET — applies ONLY to panels marked POD: Nyla's sealed glass bubble-pod, one of thousands stacked in the grey underground warren. THE POD'S ARCHITECTURE, fixed for the whole film: the FRONT of the sphere is clear glass panes held in narrow pale structural ribs -- this is the wall she presses her palm against and whispers through, the warren's stacked pods glowing faintly beyond it. The REAR AND UPPER-REAR of the sphere is a SOLID MATTE WHITE SHELL, moulded with the pod's built-in recesses: the bed alcove at frame-LEFT, the desk recess at frame-RIGHT, the potted-plant niche in the upper-LEFT wall. The two meet along pale structural ribs converging on a small hub above the pod's centre. THE HATCH IS A PERMANENT FEATURE, HUMAN-SIZED: a round door set into the sphere's frame-LEFT curve, its diameter roughly three-quarters of Nyla's standing height, sill flush with the metal catwalk walkway that runs directly outside. A small recessed release panel sits beside it. These props and this hatch appear ONLY in Pod panels; never in a Corridor panel..
CORRIDOR SET — applies ONLY to panels marked CORRIDOR: the underground warren's catwalk, visible through the open hatch in this scene: a long metal catwalk running away from camera between towering stacks of cylindrical glass pods, each holding exactly one sleeping figure in pale off-white sleepwear, motionless, flat on its bed -- only the front layer of pods reads clearly, any pods behind that front layer fall into dark shadow with no readable figures inside them, and never two figures inside one pod; wet reflective floor plating, handrails, blue-white glow receding into darkness. These elements appear ONLY in Corridor/Threshold panels; never dressed with Pod furniture..

MATERIAL MAP — fixed for this whole set: POD: The pod is PART CLEAR, PART SOLID: clear glass front (the pane she touches, the warren beyond), solid matte white rear and upper-rear shell holding the bed alcove, desk recess and plant niche, meeting at pale structural ribs with a hub above centre. Never all glass, never all white. CORRIDOR: The corridor is entirely solid: wet reflective metal catwalk decking underfoot, solid metal walls and structural framework to either side, handrails and vertical cable runs fixed to those walls. The towering stacked pods that line the corridor are separate objects, each keeping its OWN clear-glass-front/solid-white-rear boundary as seen from outside; the corridor's own walls, floor and ceiling are solid metal throughout, no transparent corridor surfaces anywhere.

SCREEN-DIRECTION LOCK — holds in EVERY panel of this sheet: Nyla holds frame-CENTRE at the glass in the early Pod panels, then owns frame-LEFT looking frame-RIGHT once she moves toward the hatch and into the corridor; key light is cold blue-white from overhead throughout. NEUTRAL permitted only on insert panels. Each character stays on their own side of the frame looking their fixed direction in every panel, even when they are only a soft foreground shoulder.

CAMERA KIT — setups covering a planted moment hold the actors still; a setup covering a moving beat (an exit, a run, a location change) is MOTION-CAPABLE and describes the move directly (holds while the subject exits frame, travels alongside, moves toward the lens) rather than freezing it into planted staging. The scene is covered by these repeated setups: A: WS, camera OUTSIDE the pod at bed height shooting in through the curved pane, the whole sphere in frame with the closed hatch visible in its frame-LEFT curve and the catwalk visible past its sill; B: MS, camera INSIDE the pod at her eye height, Nyla in the middle third; C: MCU, camera INSIDE the pod low and tilted up, her face filling the lower two-thirds; D: INSERT, camera INSIDE the pod, macro-close on one detail, no full figure; E: MS THRESHOLD, camera in the CORRIDOR at chest height looking back at the open hatch, NEUTRAL -- it legally resets the axis for the exit. Panels sharing a SETUP letter keep that same camera position, height and distance; only the action, expression and the moment advance.
