Set dressing and character blocking, identical in every shot of this scene: The Elite Viewing Hall, a private theatre high above the warren. Black ornamental walls with gold accents, arched alcoves holding lit sconces and decorative urns, a chandelier medallion glowing overhead, deep leather armchairs in a single curved front row with gold side tables and brass candleholders between them, a low brass railing at the base of the screen. Gold Woman and Old Man sit directly beside one another at the frame-LEFT end of the row -- she is on his frame-LEFT, he is on her frame-RIGHT, one gold side table between them and nobody seated between them. The three other elites sit beyond Old Man toward frame-RIGHT. The screen is a permanent feature of this set: a single curved screen the size of a building filling frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds of any wide shot, always on, always showing a live feed of the underground warren below. No character from the underground is ever physically present in this hall; they appear only as picture on the screen. Material map, fixed for this whole set: The hall is solid architecture throughout: black ornamental walls, arched alcoves, a low brass railing, deep leather armchairs -- solid materials, no transparent walls anywhere. The ONE non-solid surface in the room is the screen itself: a single curved emissive display the size of a building, filling frame-centre, always on, standing far taller than the seated elites. Every other surface (walls, floor, armchairs, side tables, railing) is solid and reflects only ambient sconce light. Screen-direction lock, identical in every shot of this scene: Gold Woman holds frame-LEFT looking frame-RIGHT toward the screen; Old Man holds frame-RIGHT of the pair looking frame-LEFT toward the screen; the screen is the shared focus at frame-CENTRE; key light is the screen itself, cold blue-white spill from frame-CENTRE, with warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT.
