This is storyboard sheet 1 of 1: a grid of 5 panels arranged in 3 columns on a plain light grey page. Each panel is a wide 16:9 cinematic frame, never square or tall. Every panel uses the same art style, Photorealistic, cinematic film still, with matching lighting and color grading throughout. Draw every character consistently across every panel, exactly as stated in the CHARACTER block above (when one is present) — never inventing or varying their look. A small plain strip below each panel shows ONLY its panel number and shot type — NOTHING else ever appears in that strip, no matter how a panel's own brief is worded below. The panel artwork carries no lettering, signs, speech bubbles, captions, dialogue or written words of any kind — keep every panel free of readable text except the small panel-number label.
CHARACTER — stated once, drawn identically in every panel: Nyla (a young woman with shoulder-length black shaggy hair…); Gold Woman (a woman in her forties, dark hair swept up, floor-length…); Old Man (an elderly man, white hair, black dinner suit); Other Elites (adults in dark formal evening wear, seated, non-speaking…)

FIXED SET — identical in EVERY panel that shows the location: The Elite Viewing Hall, a private theatre high above the warren. Black ornamental walls with gold accents, arched alcoves holding lit sconces and decorative urns, a chandelier medallion glowing overhead, deep leather armchairs in a single curved front row with gold side tables and brass candleholders between them, a low brass railing at the base of the screen. Gold Woman and Old Man sit directly beside one another at the frame-LEFT end of the row -- she is on his frame-LEFT, he is on her frame-RIGHT, one gold side table between them and nobody seated between them. The three other elites sit beyond Old Man toward frame-RIGHT. The screen is a permanent feature of this set: a single curved screen the size of a building filling frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds of any wide shot, always on, always showing a live feed of the underground warren below. No character from the underground is ever physically present in this hall; they appear only as picture on the screen.

MATERIAL MAP — fixed for this whole set: The pod is PART CLEAR, PART SOLID: clear glass front (the pane she touches, the warren beyond), solid matte white rear and upper-rear shell holding the bed alcove, desk recess and plant niche, meeting at pale structural ribs with a hub above centre. Never all glass, never all white.

SCREEN-DIRECTION LOCK — holds in EVERY panel of this sheet: Gold Woman holds frame-LEFT looking frame-RIGHT toward the screen; Old Man holds frame-RIGHT of the pair looking frame-LEFT toward the screen; the screen is the shared focus at frame-CENTRE; key light is the screen itself, cold blue-white spill from frame-CENTRE, with warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT. NEUTRAL only on the one panel marked NEUTRAL. Each character stays on their own side of the frame looking their fixed direction in every panel, even when they are only a soft foreground shoulder.

CAMERA KIT — the actors are PLANTED on the set and never move; the whole scene is covered by these repeated setups: A: WS, camera behind and above the curved front row at standing height, the row small along the lower third, the screen looming over them; B: MS, camera at seated eye height, square on Gold Woman in the middle third at frame-LEFT; C: MCU OTS over Old Man's frame-RIGHT shoulder onto Gold Woman; D: INSERT, camera square on the screen at seated eye height, the feed fills the whole panel, no hall furniture; E: MCU OTS over Gold Woman's frame-LEFT shoulder onto Old Man, the matched reverse of C; F: MCU, camera at seated eye height, square on Old Man in the middle third at frame-RIGHT; H: MS TWO-SHOT of the pair, camera placed just forward of the row and slightly to its frame-LEFT, shooting back across the pair so both faces are readable at the same size, screen behind camera so it is NOT visible in this panel; G: NEUTRAL WS, camera at the screen's own position looking back into the hall at standing height. Panels whose brief carries the same SETUP letter are the SAME camera position and the SAME frozen staging repeated EXACTLY — copy the framing, body positions, distance and orientation from the first panel with that letter; only faces, gestures and the caption change between them.

CONSTRAINTS: never swap which side of the frame a character occupies; never mirror or flip a panel; one action per panel; a panel may break the screen-direction lock only if its brief says NEUTRAL; never repeat an earlier panel of this sheet identically — escalate closer or wider instead; write every panel brief as ordinary prose, never as a labelled heading or a "WORD: ..." directive line — the caption strip below each panel shows ONLY its number and shot type, and nothing else ever appears there.
