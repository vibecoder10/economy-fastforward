Set dressing, identical in every panel of this scene tagged 'Pod' (never the dressing of Corridor): Nyla's sealed glass bubble-pod, one of thousands stacked in the grey underground warren. THE POD'S ARCHITECTURE, fixed for the whole film: the FRONT of the sphere is clear glass panes held in narrow pale structural ribs -- this is the wall she presses her palm against and whispers through, the warren's stacked pods glowing faintly beyond it. The REAR AND UPPER-REAR of the sphere is a SOLID MATTE WHITE SHELL, moulded with the pod's built-in recesses: the bed alcove at frame-LEFT, the desk recess at frame-RIGHT, the potted-plant niche in the upper-LEFT wall. The two meet along pale structural ribs converging on a small hub above the pod's centre. THE HATCH IS A PERMANENT FEATURE, HUMAN-SIZED: a round door set into the sphere's frame-LEFT curve, its diameter roughly three-quarters of Nyla's standing height, sill flush with the metal catwalk walkway that runs directly outside. A small recessed release panel sits beside it. These props and this hatch appear ONLY in Pod panels; never in a Corridor panel. Material map, fixed for this whole set: POD: The pod is PART CLEAR, PART SOLID: clear glass front (the pane she touches, the warren beyond), solid matte white rear and upper-rear shell holding the bed alcove, desk recess and plant niche, meeting at pale structural ribs with a hub above centre. Never all glass, never all white. CORRIDOR: The corridor is entirely solid: wet reflective metal catwalk decking underfoot, solid metal walls and structural framework to either side, handrails and vertical cable runs fixed to those walls. The towering stacked pods that line the corridor are separate objects, each keeping its OWN clear-glass-front/solid-white-rear boundary as seen from outside; the corridor's own walls, floor and ceiling are solid metal throughout, no transparent corridor surfaces anywhere. Screen-direction lock, identical in every shot of this scene: Nyla holds frame-CENTRE at the glass in the early Pod panels, then owns frame-LEFT looking frame-RIGHT once she moves toward the hatch and into the corridor; key light is cold blue-white from overhead throughout. NEUTRAL permitted only on insert panels.. Never mirror or flip the composition. Camera kit for this scene — setups covering a planted conversation hold the actors still; a setup covering a moving beat (a BRIDGE, an exit, a run) is MOTION-CAPABLE instead and describes the move directly (exits frame-right and camera holds, travels alongside, runs toward the lens) — never freeze a moving beat into planted-body staging.
