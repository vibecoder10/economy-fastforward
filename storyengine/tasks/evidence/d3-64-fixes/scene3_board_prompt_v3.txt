STORYBOARD SHEET — SCENE 3 — 9 PANELS  (v3)

This is a storyboard sheet: a grid of 9 panels in 3 columns and 3 rows on a plain light grey page. Each panel is a wide 16:9 cinematic frame — never square, never tall. Every panel uses the same art style: photorealistic cinematic film still, with matching lighting and colour grading throughout. A small plain strip below each panel shows only its panel number and shot type. The panel artwork contains no lettering, signs, speech bubbles, captions or written words of any kind — only the small panel-number label.

CHARACTER — stated once, drawn identically in every panel: Nyla, a young woman with shoulder-length black shaggy hair, wearing a charcoal grey zip-up bodysuit. She is ALONE in this scene: exactly one person in every panel, no other figures anywhere, and no sleeping bodies visible inside her own pod.

SET — every panel takes place at Nyla's bubble-pod. THE POD'S ARCHITECTURE, fixed for the whole film and identical in every scene: a sealed sphere that is PART CLEAR AND PART SOLID. The FRONT of the sphere, facing the catwalk, is clear glass panes held in narrow pale structural ribs — this is the wall she presses her palm against and looks out through, and the warren's stacked pods glow faintly beyond it. The REAR AND UPPER-REAR of the sphere is a SOLID MATTE WHITE SHELL, moulded with the pod's built-in recesses: the bed alcove at frame-LEFT, the desk recess at frame-RIGHT, the potted-plant niche in the upper-LEFT wall. The two meet along pale structural ribs that converge on a small hub directly above the centre of the pod. Solid white behind and above her, clear glass in front of her — never all glass, and never all white.
THE POD INTERIOR: Upholstered bed frame-LEFT against the curved wall, rounded armchair CENTRE-RIGHT beside a low cylindrical coffee table, desk with storage FAR RIGHT against the curve, circular floor mat CENTRE, potted plant in the upper-LEFT wall niche, small glowing sphere lamp on the coffee table. A human-sized round hatch sits closed in the frame-LEFT curve, its sill flush with the catwalk outside. Cold blue-white LED light from the dome overhead.
THE CEILING is the subject of this scene and must be visible in every interior panel that is not a macro insert. It follows the architecture above: directly over her the SOLID MATTE WHITE SHELL curves overhead from the rear of the pod, carrying small flush fittings — vents, light strips, sensor points — all the same pale white, all unremarkable; forward of the rib hub the ceiling becomes clear glass through which the dark warren and the faint glow of neighbouring pods show. Anything mounted overhead lives on the solid white shell or on the pale ribs, never floating on the glass.
THE WARREN OUTSIDE, visible only through the glass and in the final exterior panel: towering stacks of other cylindrical pods glowing faintly, wet reflective catwalk plating, blue-white glow receding into darkness.

THE LENS — READ THIS CAREFULLY, IT IS THE POINT OF THE SCENE: set flush into the SOLID MATTE WHITE CEILING SHELL just behind the rib hub, directly above the centre of the pod, there is a single small dark glass bead — a hidden camera lens, about the size of a fingertip, its dark circle the only dark point on that whole pale white surface, with a faint ring of reflected light at its rim. It is mounted in the solid white shell, never floating on the glass.
IT MUST BE PLANTED WITHOUT BEING ANNOUNCED. In the panels before she finds it, the bead is genuinely present and genuinely visible in the dome — but it is NOT emphasised in any way: do not light it, do not make it glow, do not centre the composition on it, do not enlarge it, do not add a red indicator. It is one small dark point among the dome's ordinary fittings, easy to miss on a first look and unmistakable on a second. From the panel where she notices it onward, it stays exactly the same size and in exactly the same seam — the shot sizes change, the lens never does.

SCREEN DIRECTION — holds across the whole sheet: the dome and its lens are ABOVE her, so her attention travels UP; when she turns away from it she turns toward frame-LEFT and the bed; when she turns back she turns toward frame-RIGHT and looks up. Key light is the cold blue-white dome light from above. Never mirror or flip a panel. Only a panel marked NEUTRAL may reset this.

CAMERA KIT — every panel names its setup. A setup fixes the camera's side of the glass, its height and its distance; the action inside it progresses from panel to panel. Nyla moves within this one location: the fixed-camera rule applies to each setup, not to her body.
A — WS, camera INSIDE the pod at bed height, wide enough to hold Nyla standing and the dome ceiling curving above her, the furniture in its locked positions across the frame.
B — MCU, camera INSIDE the pod at her eye height, square on her, she occupies the middle third; the curved pale dome soft behind and above her head.
C — LOW MS, camera INSIDE the pod near the floor tilted UP: Nyla's head and shoulders across the lower third, the dome ceiling filling the upper two-thirds with the solid white shell behind, the rib hub, the glass forward of it, and the lens bead among the shell's fittings.
D — INSERT MACRO, camera INSIDE the pod, macro-close on the solid white ceiling shell itself, no figure in frame at all.
E — LENS POV, NEUTRAL: the camera occupies the LENS'S OWN POSITION, set in the solid white ceiling shell just behind the rib hub, directly above the centre of the pod, looking straight DOWN into the room. Nyla is below, small-to-medium in frame and centred, foreshortened by the height, her face tilted up. What surrounds her in this view is the pod FLOOR — the circular mat, the coffee table and its glowing sphere lamp, the bed and armchair splayed outward around her — never the dome, which is where this camera is. A faint barrel distortion at the frame edges, like a security lens. This is the only panel that may break the screen-direction lock.
F — MS FROM BESIDE THE BED, camera INSIDE the pod at her shoulder height, level and square, holding her whole upper body in the middle third with the bed at frame-LEFT and the dome curving above.
G — WS, camera OUTSIDE the pod at bed height, shooting in through the curved pane: the whole sphere in frame with the dark warren stacks around it, the closed human-sized hatch visible in its frame-LEFT curve with the catwalk running past its sill, Nyla small inside.

DRAW THESE PANELS IN ORDER:

[1] POD · WS · (SETUP A) — Nyla stands alone near the centre of the pod, arms loose at her sides, chest still settling from breath, occupying about a fifth of the frame; the furniture holds its locked positions around her and the pale dome curves overhead, the solid white shell curving overhead behind her with its small flush fittings ordinary and unremarked, clear glass forward of the rib hub with the warren's faint glow beyond it — the dark bead is present on the white shell just behind the hub, directly above the pod's centre, no larger and no brighter than the fittings beside it. Her face is three-quarter to the lens, eyes level and unfocused. Nothing in the composition points at the dome.

[2] POD · LOW MS · (SETUP C) — the first notice: Nyla's head and shoulders across the lower third, face tilted UP and three-quarter to the lens, eyes narrowing as they find something; the dome fills the upper two-thirds and the small dark bead sits on the solid white shell just behind the rib hub, in the same place on the white shell and at the same size as panel 1, still unlit and unemphasised — it is simply now in the same frame as her gaze.

[3] POD · INSERT MACRO · (SETUP D) — the lens itself, no figure in frame: a dark glass bead about the size of a fingertip, set flush into the solid matte white ceiling shell, a faint ring of reflected light at its rim, a little dust caught in the moulded seam beside it, the pale shell falling away out of focus around it. Cold, still, patient. Nothing else in the panel.

[4] POD · MCU · (SETUP B) — the instinct: Nyla square in the middle third at her eye height, three-quarter to the lens, eyes wide and breath caught, her near shoulder already lifting toward the dome as if to put something between herself and it; the pale dome soft behind her head.

[5] POD · MS · (SETUP F) — the instinct completed: Nyla has turned toward frame-LEFT and the bed, her BACK now to camera and to the dome, face not visible, head lowered between her shoulders, one hand half-raised near her collarbone; her feet stay where they were and she has not walked away — only her upper body has turned. The bed sits at frame-LEFT, the dome curves above, the bead still on the white shell.

[6] POD · MCU · (SETUP B) — the same camera as panel 4, the moment progressed: she has stopped and come back square to the room, three-quarter to the lens again, eyes dropping once and then lifting, jaw setting — the decision landing in her face and nowhere else. Her shoulder has come down. The pale dome soft behind her.

[7] POD · LOW MS · (SETUP C) — the same camera as panel 2, the moment progressed: Nyla has straightened to her full height with her chin lifted, head and shoulders across the lower third, face tilted UP and now nearly to-lens, looking directly at the bead; the dome fills the upper two-thirds, the bead unchanged on the white shell. She is not hiding.

[8] LENS POV · NEUTRAL · (SETUP E) — the pivot of the film, seen from the lens's own position in the dome looking straight down: Nyla stands centred below, foreshortened, her face tilted fully up and SQUARE TO THE LENS with her eyes looking directly into camera, no flinch and no three-quarter — she is looking back. Around and beneath her the pod floor splays outward: the circular mat, the coffee table with its glowing sphere lamp, the bed at one side and the armchair at the other. Faint barrel distortion at the frame edges like a security feed. The ceiling is NOT visible in this panel, because this camera is mounted in it.

[9] POD · WS FROM OUTSIDE · (SETUP G) — from the catwalk outside, shooting in through the curved pane: the whole sphere sits among the dark stacks of other glowing pods, the closed hatch in its frame-LEFT curve with the catwalk running past the sill, and Nyla is small inside, standing at the centre with her face still tilted up toward the dome, holding the look. Her face is not readable at this size — her posture is. The warren stretches away into darkness around her.

CONSTRAINTS: one action per panel; never mirror or flip a panel; exactly one person in every panel and no other figures anywhere; never swap which side of the frame the bed, armchair, desk or hatch occupies; the lens bead stays the same size, the same darkness and in the same place on the solid white ceiling shell in every panel that shows it, and is never lit, never glowing, never centred and never enlarged before she notices it; when Nyla turns, only her upper body turns unless a panel says she walks — her feet and the furniture never re-stage; the ceiling is visible in every interior panel except the macro insert and the lens-POV panel; in the lens-POV panel the ceiling is never visible because the camera is mounted in it; the pod is always part solid white shell (rear and above) and part clear glass (front), never all one or the other; keep every panel free of readable text except its number label.
