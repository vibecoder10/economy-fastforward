STORYBOARD SHEET — SCENE 5 — 9 PANELS  (v3)

This is a storyboard sheet: a grid of 9 panels in 3 columns and 3 rows on a plain light grey page. Each panel is a wide 16:9 cinematic frame — never square, never tall. Every panel uses the same art style: photorealistic cinematic film still, with matching lighting and colour grading throughout. A small plain strip below each panel shows only its panel number and shot type — nothing else ever appears in that strip: no instruction text, no notes about arrangement or camera, no descriptions. The panel artwork contains no lettering, signs, speech bubbles, captions or written words of any kind — only the small panel-number label.

CAST — stated once, drawn identically in every panel. There are EXACTLY FIVE elites in this hall, never four and never six:
GOLD WOMAN — a woman in her forties, dark hair swept up, floor-length gold gown, holding a wine glass.
OLD MAN — an elderly man, white hair, black dinner suit. He sits DIRECTLY BESIDE HER, on her frame-RIGHT, with one gold side table between them and nobody seated between them.
THREE OTHER ELITES — adults in dark formal evening wear, seated beyond the OLD MAN toward frame-RIGHT, non-speaking; they only react.
NYLA appears in this scene ONLY as picture on the screen, never physically in the hall: a young woman with shoulder-length black shaggy hair in a charcoal grey bodysuit.

SET — every panel takes place in THE ELITE VIEWING HALL, a private theatre high above the warren: black ornamental walls with gold accents, arched alcoves holding lit sconces and decorative urns, a chandelier medallion glowing overhead, deep leather armchairs in a single curved front row with gold side tables and brass candleholders between them, a low brass railing at the base of the screen. Warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT.
THE SCREEN IS A PERMANENT FEATURE OF THIS SET: a single curved screen the size of a building, filling frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds of any wide shot, its base behind the brass railing, standing far taller than the seated figures so the elites read as small in front of it. It is always ON and always the room's brightest source.

WHAT IS ON THE SCREEN IN THIS SCENE — the same image in every panel that shows the feed, and it must not drift: the view from a hidden lens mounted in the ceiling of Nyla's pod, looking straight DOWN at her. She stands centred below, foreshortened by the height, her face tilted fully up and SQUARE TO THE LENS, eyes looking directly into it. Around and beneath her the pod floor splays outward — a circular mat, a low coffee table with a glowing sphere lamp, a bed to one side and an armchair to the other. Faint barrel distortion at the edges, like a security feed. She is not moving and she is not looking away.
CONTINUITY WITH THE PREVIOUS SCENE: this is the identical view the previous scene ended on, seen from the identical position. Panels 1 and 7 must repeat that framing frame for frame — same height, same foreshortening, same distortion, same objects splayed around her — so that the cut into this scene lands as the same image in a new context. Do not re-stage her, do not change the angle, do not zoom.

SCREEN DIRECTION — holds across the whole sheet: GOLD WOMAN holds the frame-LEFT position of the pair and looks frame-RIGHT toward the screen; OLD MAN holds the frame-RIGHT position and looks frame-LEFT toward the screen; the screen is the shared focus at frame-CENTRE. Key light is the screen itself — cold blue-white spill from frame-CENTRE washing every face — with warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT. Each character keeps their frame side and looking direction in every panel. Never mirror or flip a panel. Only a panel marked NEUTRAL may reset this.

CAMERA KIT — every panel names its setup. A setup fixes the camera's position, height and distance; the action inside it progresses from panel to panel. The elites are seated and stay seated: their chairs, hips and knees stay pointed at the screen in every panel, and only their heads, shoulders and upper bodies move.
A — WS, camera behind and above the curved front row at standing height: the row of elites small along the lower third, the screen looming over them filling frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds.
B — MS, camera at seated eye height, square on GOLD WOMAN who occupies the middle third at frame-LEFT, screen glow raking her from frame-RIGHT.
C — MCU OTS, camera at seated eye height over OLD MAN's frame-RIGHT shoulder onto GOLD WOMAN: his dark shoulder and white hair soft in the frame-RIGHT foreground corner, her face sharp in the middle third at frame-LEFT.
D — INSERT, camera square on the screen at seated eye height: the feed fills the whole panel edge to edge, no hall furniture and no people of the hall in frame.
E — MCU OTS, the matched reverse of C at the same size: camera over GOLD WOMAN's frame-LEFT shoulder onto OLD MAN, her gold shoulder soft in the frame-LEFT foreground corner, his face sharp in the middle third at frame-RIGHT.
F — MCU, camera at seated eye height, square on OLD MAN who occupies the middle third at frame-RIGHT, screen glow raking him from frame-LEFT.
G — NEUTRAL WS, camera at the screen's own position looking back into the hall at standing height. Because this camera faces the row from the opposite side, the seating order READS REVERSED left-to-right compared with every front-on setup, and that reversed order must be stated in the panel that uses it: the whole curved row of five elites small and centred, their faces lit cold blue by the screen behind camera, the chandelier and alcoves receding into the dark above them. Because the camera stands where the screen is, the screen itself is NOT visible in this panel. This is the only panel that may break the screen-direction lock.
I — TIGHT SCREEN INSERT, camera in the hall but moved CLOSER to the screen at seated eye height, so the central portion of the same feed fills the panel: Nyla's upturned face large in frame, the barrel distortion now curving at the edges of her face, the pod floor objects pushed out of frame by the tighter framing. Same feed, same view, same moment — only the camera's distance to the screen has changed.
H — MS TWO-SHOT OF THE PAIR, camera at seated eye height placed just forward of the row and slightly to its frame-LEFT, shooting back across the pair toward frame-RIGHT so BOTH faces are readable in the same frame at the same size: GOLD WOMAN nearest camera in the frame-LEFT half, OLD MAN just beyond her in the frame-RIGHT half, the gold side table between them, their shoulders almost touching. BACKGROUND FOR THIS CAMERA POSITION: the camera stands BETWEEN the row and the screen, so the screen is BEHIND CAMERA and must NOT appear in this panel — its cool blue light falls on their faces frontally, and what sits behind them is the hall's curved back wall with its arched alcoves, warm sconces and the chandelier medallion above.

DRAW THESE PANELS IN ORDER:

[1] SCREEN · INSERT · (SETUP D) — the feed alone, filling the panel edge to edge, repeating the previous scene's final view frame for frame: looking straight down from the ceiling lens at Nyla standing centred below, foreshortened, face tilted fully up and square to the lens, eyes directly into it; the pod floor splayed around her with the circular mat, the coffee table and its glowing sphere lamp, the bed to one side and the armchair to the other; faint barrel distortion at the edges. No hall furniture and nobody of the hall in frame.

[2] HALL · WS · (SETUP A) — the reveal of context: the same feed, now seen as the giant screen it is, filling frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds; below it EXACTLY FIVE elites sit small along the lower third in their curved row, GOLD WOMAN and OLD MAN side by side at the frame-LEFT end with nobody between them and the three others beyond him at frame-RIGHT. All five face the screen. No faces to the lens — the row is seen from behind and above.

[3] HALL · MS · (SETUP B) — GOLD WOMAN fills the middle third at frame-LEFT, three-quarter to the lens, her wine glass stopped halfway to her mouth, eyes narrowing as the smile goes out of her face; the screen's cold blue glow rakes the frame-RIGHT side of her face.

[4] HALL · MCU OTS · (SETUP C) — over OLD MAN's frame-RIGHT shoulder: his dark shoulder and white hair soft in the frame-RIGHT foreground corner, GOLD WOMAN's face sharp in the middle third at frame-LEFT, three-quarter to the lens, lowering the glass out of frame, lips parting — no amusement left in it.

[5] HALL · MS TWO-SHOT · (SETUP H) — the lean, and the shot that holds the pair: GOLD WOMAN in the frame-LEFT half and OLD MAN just beyond her in the frame-RIGHT half of the SAME frame, seated side by side with only the gold side table between them, both at the same size and both in focus. Their chairs, hips and knees stay pointed at the screen exactly as in panel 2 — no chair is turned — but both have come FORWARD off the chair backs, shoulders leaning toward the screen and out over their knees, her glass forgotten in her hand. The screen is behind camera and not visible; its cool blue light hits their faces frontally while the hall's alcoves and warm sconces sit behind them.

[6] HALL · MCU OTS · (SETUP E) — the matched reverse of panel 4 at the same size: over GOLD WOMAN's frame-LEFT shoulder, her gold shoulder soft in the frame-LEFT foreground corner, OLD MAN's face sharp in the middle third at frame-RIGHT, three-quarter to the lens, leaning in, the comfortable smile gone and something sharper behind his eyes.

[7] SCREEN · TIGHT INSERT · (SETUP I) — the same feed, the same downward view, the same moment — but the camera has moved closer to the screen so her upturned face now fills the panel: eyes directly into the lens, barrel distortion curving at the edges of her face, the pod floor pushed out of frame. Nothing about her has been re-staged and nothing about the view has changed; only our distance to it has. This panel escalates on panel 1 rather than repeating it.

[8] HALL · NEUTRAL WS · (SETUP G) — from the screen's own position looking back into the hall, where five seated figures fill the curved row: three of the other elites sit together at frame-LEFT, the old man sits to their right of centre, and the gold woman sits furthest at frame-RIGHT, her gown the one bright thing in the row — five people, all of them present in frame. Every face is lit cold blue from behind camera, every one of them leaning forward now, glasses lowered, nobody laughing. The chandelier medallion and the arched alcoves recede into darkness above and behind them. The screen is not visible, because the camera stands where it is. We are watching them from where she is.

[9] HALL · MCU · (SETUP F) — OLD MAN alone fills the middle third at frame-RIGHT, three-quarter to the lens, eyes frame-LEFT on the screen, still leaning forward; the cold expression of a man who has just been genuinely interested for the first time in years — not amused, awake. Blue screen-light rakes the frame-LEFT side of his face, warm sconce light behind him at frame-RIGHT.

CONSTRAINTS: one action per panel; never mirror or flip a panel; GOLD WOMAN stays frame-LEFT of OLD MAN in every panel except the panel marked NEUTRAL, and the two are always seated adjacent with nobody between them; the two over-the-shoulder panels (4 and 6) are the same shot size over opposite shoulders, and panel 5's two-shot must agree with them by showing the pair seated adjacent; the hall always contains EXACTLY FIVE elites in every panel where the group is visible, never one more and never one fewer; every elite stays seated with chairs, hips and knees pointed at the screen and no chair is ever re-oriented — leaning and attention move the upper body only; a setup that places the camera between the row and the screen never shows the screen behind them, only its light on their faces; panel 1 shows the identical feed framing as the previous scene's final view and must match it exactly, and panel 7 shows that same view only closer — never a duplicate panel; wherever a group appears, state the arrangement as seen from THAT camera position, remembering that a reverse angle reads its order backwards, and draw every member of the stated count; Nyla never appears physically in the hall; keep every panel free of readable text except its number label.
