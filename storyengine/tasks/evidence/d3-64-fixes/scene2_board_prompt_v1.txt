STORYBOARD SHEET — SCENE 2 — 9 PANELS

This is a storyboard sheet: a grid of 9 panels in 3 columns and 3 rows on a plain light grey page. Each panel is a wide 16:9 cinematic frame — never square, never tall. Every panel uses the same art style: photorealistic cinematic film still, with matching lighting and colour grading throughout. A small plain strip below each panel shows only its panel number and shot type. The panel artwork contains no lettering, signs, speech bubbles, captions or written words of any kind — only the small panel-number label.

CAST — stated once, drawn identically in every panel:
GOLD WOMAN — a woman in her forties, dark hair swept up, floor-length gold gown, holding a wine glass.
OLD MAN — an elderly man, white hair, black dinner suit.
THREE OTHER ELITES — adults in dark formal evening wear, seated, non-speaking; they only react.

SET — applies to every panel in this scene, which all take place in one location. THE ELITE VIEWING HALL: a private theatre high above the warren. Black ornamental walls with gold accents, arched alcoves holding lit sconces and decorative urns, a chandelier medallion glowing overhead, deep leather armchairs in a single curved front row with gold side tables and brass candleholders between them, a low brass railing at the base of the screen. Warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT.
THE SCREEN IS A PERMANENT FEATURE OF THIS SET: a single curved screen the size of a building, filling the frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds of any wide shot, its base behind the brass railing, standing far taller than the seated figures — the elites read as small in front of it. It is always ON, always the room's brightest source, and it always shows a live feed of the underground warren below. No character from the underground is ever physically present in this hall; they appear only as picture on that screen.

WHAT IS ON THE SCREEN — specify it in every panel where the screen is visible, so the feed never drifts: a vast grid of stacked transparent bubble-pods glowing faintly in a dark warren, hundreds of them, a small figure asleep or alone inside each. In panels that call for it, ONE pod is picked out nearer the centre of the feed: NYLA, a young woman with shoulder-length black shaggy hair in a charcoal grey bodysuit, standing with one palm raised flat against the curve of her pod.

SCREEN DIRECTION — holds across the whole sheet: GOLD WOMAN sits frame-LEFT and looks frame-RIGHT toward the screen; OLD MAN sits frame-RIGHT and looks frame-LEFT toward the screen; the screen is the shared focus at frame-CENTRE. Key light is the screen itself — cold blue-white spill from frame-CENTRE washing every face — with warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT. Each character keeps their frame side and looking direction in every panel, even when they are only a soft foreground shoulder. Never mirror or flip a panel. Only a panel marked NEUTRAL may reset this.

CAMERA KIT — every panel names its setup. A setup fixes the camera's position, height and distance; the action inside it progresses from panel to panel. The elites are seated and stay seated: within this location the staging is planted, and only faces, gestures and glasses move.
A — WS, camera behind and above the curved front row at standing height: the row of elites small along the lower third, the screen looming over them filling frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds.
B — MS, camera at seated eye height, square on GOLD WOMAN who occupies the middle third at frame-LEFT, screen glow raking her from frame-RIGHT.
C — MCU OTS, camera at seated eye height over OLD MAN's frame-RIGHT shoulder onto GOLD WOMAN: his dark shoulder and white hair soft in the frame-RIGHT foreground corner, her face sharp in the middle third at frame-LEFT.
D — INSERT, camera square on the screen at seated eye height: the feed fills the whole panel edge to edge, no hall furniture, no people in the room.
E — MCU OTS, the matched reverse of C at the same size: camera over GOLD WOMAN's frame-LEFT shoulder onto OLD MAN, her gold shoulder and swept hair soft in the frame-LEFT foreground corner, his face sharp in the middle third at frame-RIGHT.
F — MCU, camera at seated eye height, square on OLD MAN who occupies the middle third at frame-RIGHT, screen glow raking him from frame-LEFT.
G — NEUTRAL WS, camera at the screen's own position looking back into the hall at standing height: the whole curved row of elites small and centred, their faces lit cold blue by the screen behind camera, the chandelier and alcoves receding into the dark above them. This is the only panel that may break the screen-direction lock.

DRAW THESE PANELS IN ORDER:

[1] HALL · WS · (SETUP A) — establish: the five elites seated along the lower third in their curved row, glasses in hand, all faces turned to the screen; the screen fills frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds showing the vast grid of glowing bubble-pods with a small figure inside each. GOLD WOMAN at frame-LEFT, OLD MAN at frame-RIGHT, three other elites between them. No faces to the lens — the whole row is seen from behind and above.

[2] HALL · MS · (SETUP B) — GOLD WOMAN fills the middle third at frame-LEFT, three-quarter to the lens, lifting her wine glass and pointing it frame-RIGHT toward the screen, lips parted mid-sentence, amused; the screen's cold blue glow rakes the frame-RIGHT side of her face and the dark hall recedes behind her.

[3] SCREEN · INSERT · (SETUP D) — the feed fills the whole panel: the grid of stacked bubble-pods, and picked out nearer the centre one lit pod holding NYLA standing with one palm raised flat against the curve of her pod, tiny in the vast grid. No hall furniture and no elites in frame.

[4] HALL · MCU OTS · (SETUP C) — over OLD MAN's frame-RIGHT shoulder: his dark shoulder and white hair soft in the frame-RIGHT foreground corner, GOLD WOMAN's face sharp in the middle third at frame-LEFT, three-quarter to the lens, smiling as she finishes speaking, her glass still raised at the frame edge.

[5] HALL · WS · (SETUP A) — the same camera and same row as panel 1, the moment progressed: the room laughing softly — heads tilted toward one another, shoulders loose, two glasses raised; the screen behind them unchanged, still showing the grid of pods with Nyla's raised palm in the picked-out pod near its centre.

[6] HALL · MCU OTS · (SETUP E) — the matched reverse of panel 4 at the same size: over GOLD WOMAN's frame-LEFT shoulder, her gold shoulder and swept-up hair soft in the frame-LEFT foreground corner, OLD MAN's face sharp in the middle third at frame-RIGHT, three-quarter to the lens, smiling faintly as he speaks.

[7] HALL · MCU · (SETUP F) — OLD MAN alone fills the middle third at frame-RIGHT, three-quarter to the lens, eyes frame-LEFT on the screen, the smile settling into something colder and more comfortable; blue screen-light rakes the frame-LEFT side of his face, warm sconce light behind him at frame-RIGHT.

[8] SCREEN · INSERT · (SETUP D) — the feed fills the whole panel again, pulled wider than panel 3 so hundreds of lit pods stretch to every edge, each with its own small figure inside, Nyla now only one of the many and no longer picked out; along the very bottom edge of the panel the silhouetted heads of the elites' front row sit small and dark against the glow — the cast, and the audience, in one frame.

[9] HALL · NEUTRAL WS · (SETUP G) — from the screen's own position looking back into the hall: the curved row of elites small and centred, every face lit cold blue from behind camera, glasses in hand, mid-laugh; the chandelier medallion and the arched alcoves recede into darkness above and behind them. The hall is watching us; we are standing where the pods are.

CONSTRAINTS: one action per panel; never mirror or flip a panel; GOLD WOMAN stays frame-LEFT and OLD MAN stays frame-RIGHT in every panel except the panel marked NEUTRAL; the two over-the-shoulder panels (4 and 6) are the same shot size over opposite shoulders; no character from the underground ever appears physically in the hall, only as picture on the screen; the screen is on and is the brightest source in every panel that shows it; state nothing on the screen that this prompt has not specified; keep every panel free of readable text except its number label.
