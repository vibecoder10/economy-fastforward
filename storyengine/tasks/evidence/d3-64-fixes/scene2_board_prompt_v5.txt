STORYBOARD SHEET — SCENE 2 — 9 PANELS  (v5)

This is a storyboard sheet: a grid of 9 panels in 3 columns and 3 rows on a plain light grey page. Each panel is a wide 16:9 cinematic frame — never square, never tall. Every panel uses the same art style: photorealistic cinematic film still, with matching lighting and colour grading throughout. A small plain strip below each panel shows only its panel number and shot type. The panel artwork contains no lettering, signs, speech bubbles, captions or written words of any kind — only the small panel-number label.

CAST — stated once, drawn identically in every panel:
GOLD WOMAN — a woman in her forties, dark hair swept up, floor-length gold gown, holding a wine glass.
OLD MAN — an elderly man, white hair, black dinner suit.
THREE OTHER ELITES — adults in dark formal evening wear, seated, non-speaking; they only react.

SET — applies to every panel in this scene, which all take place in one location. THE ELITE VIEWING HALL: a private theatre high above the warren. Black ornamental walls with gold accents, arched alcoves holding lit sconces and decorative urns, a chandelier medallion glowing overhead, deep leather armchairs in a single curved front row with gold side tables and brass candleholders between them, a low brass railing at the base of the screen. SEATING, fixed for the whole scene: GOLD WOMAN and OLD MAN sit DIRECTLY BESIDE ONE ANOTHER at the frame-LEFT end of the row — she is on his frame-LEFT, he is on her frame-RIGHT, one gold side table between them and nobody seated between them. The three other elites sit beyond the OLD MAN toward frame-RIGHT. Any two-character shot of the pair must match this adjacency: they are neighbours, never separated by other people. Warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT.
THE SCREEN IS A PERMANENT FEATURE OF THIS SET: a single curved screen the size of a building, filling the frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds of any wide shot, its base behind the brass railing, standing far taller than the seated figures — the elites read as small in front of it. It is always ON, always the room's brightest source, and it always shows a live feed of the underground warren below. No character from the underground is ever physically present in this hall; they appear only as picture on that screen.

WHAT IS ON THE SCREEN — specify it in every panel where the screen is visible, so the feed never drifts: a vast grid of stacked transparent bubble-pods glowing faintly in a dark warren, hundreds of them. THE POPULATION, identical in every panel that shows the feed: inside each pod lies EXACTLY ONE sleeping figure in pale off-white sleepwear, motionless, flat on its bed — a grid of pale still bodies, one body per pod and never two. Only the front layer of pods reads clearly; any pods behind that front layer fall away into dark blue shadow with no readable figures inside them. Never stack readable bodies in depth behind one another, and never let two figures appear inside a single pod. In panels that call for it, ONE pod is picked out nearer the centre of the feed: NYLA, a young woman with shoulder-length black shaggy hair in a CHARCOAL GREY bodysuit, standing upright with one palm raised flat against the curve of her pod. She is the only dark figure in the grid, the only one standing and the only one awake, and that is what makes the eye find her — never dress the sleepers in dark clothing, and never draw Nyla lying down or in pale clothing.

SCREEN DIRECTION — holds across the whole sheet: GOLD WOMAN holds the frame-LEFT position and looks frame-RIGHT toward the screen; OLD MAN holds the frame-RIGHT position of the pair and looks frame-LEFT toward the screen (they are neighbours, so in two-character shots he is simply the one on her right); the screen is the shared focus at frame-CENTRE. Key light is the screen itself — cold blue-white spill from frame-CENTRE washing every face — with warm amber sconce light from screen-LEFT. Each character keeps their frame side and looking direction in every panel, even when they are only a soft foreground shoulder. Never mirror or flip a panel. Only a panel marked NEUTRAL may reset this.

CAMERA KIT — every panel names its setup. A setup fixes the camera's position, height and distance; the action inside it progresses from panel to panel. The elites are seated and stay seated: within this location the staging is planted, and only faces, gestures and glasses move.
A — WS, camera behind and above the curved front row at standing height: the row of elites small along the lower third, the screen looming over them filling frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds.
B — MS, camera at seated eye height, square on GOLD WOMAN who occupies the middle third at frame-LEFT, screen glow raking her from frame-RIGHT.
C — MCU OTS, camera at seated eye height over OLD MAN's frame-RIGHT shoulder onto GOLD WOMAN: his dark shoulder and white hair soft in the frame-RIGHT foreground corner, her face sharp in the middle third at frame-LEFT.
D — INSERT, camera square on the screen at seated eye height: the feed fills the whole panel edge to edge, no hall furniture, no people in the room.
E — MCU OTS, the matched reverse of C at the same size: camera over GOLD WOMAN's frame-LEFT shoulder onto OLD MAN, her gold shoulder and swept hair soft in the frame-LEFT foreground corner, his face sharp in the middle third at frame-RIGHT.
F — MCU, camera at seated eye height, square on OLD MAN who occupies the middle third at frame-RIGHT, screen glow raking him from frame-LEFT.
H — MS TWO-SHOT OF THE PAIR, camera at seated eye height placed just forward of the row and slightly to its frame-LEFT, shooting back across the pair toward frame-RIGHT so BOTH faces are readable in the same frame at the same size: GOLD WOMAN nearest camera in the frame-LEFT half, OLD MAN just beyond her in the frame-RIGHT half, the gold side table between them, their shoulders almost touching. ORIENTATION LOCK, and it is the point of this setup: both of them remain SEATED SQUARELY FACING THE SCREEN exactly as in panel 1 — chairs, hips and knees unchanged and still pointed at the screen — and ONLY their heads and shoulders turn toward each other. Never re-orient the armchairs, never seat the pair face-to-face like two people at a dinner table, never place a table between them as if they were dining. BACKGROUND FOR THIS CAMERA POSITION: because the camera stands BETWEEN the row and the screen, the screen is BEHIND CAMERA and must NOT appear in this panel at all. Its cool blue light falls on their faces frontally, from behind camera. What sits behind them is the hall itself — the curved back wall with its arched alcoves, warm sconces, and the chandelier medallion glowing high above. Never draw the screen or the pods behind the elites in this setup. This is the shot that proves the two are neighbours — their adjacency must be unmistakable in it.
G — NEUTRAL WS, camera at the screen's own position looking back into the hall at standing height: the whole curved row of elites small and centred, their faces lit cold blue by the screen behind camera, the chandelier and alcoves receding into the dark above them. This is the only panel that may break the screen-direction lock.

DRAW THESE PANELS IN ORDER:

[1] HALL · WS · (SETUP A) — establish: EXACTLY FIVE elites — GOLD WOMAN, OLD MAN and three others, never four and never six — seated along the lower third in their curved row, glasses in hand, all faces turned to the screen; the screen fills frame-CENTRE and the upper two-thirds showing the grid of glowing bubble-pods, each holding one pale sleeping figure. GOLD WOMAN and OLD MAN sit side by side at the frame-LEFT end of the row with one gold side table between them and nobody between them; the three other elites sit beyond him toward frame-RIGHT. No faces to the lens — the whole row is seen from behind and above, so their adjacency reads clearly.

[2] HALL · MS · (SETUP B) — GOLD WOMAN fills the middle third at frame-LEFT, three-quarter to the lens, lifting her wine glass and pointing it frame-RIGHT toward the screen, lips parted mid-sentence, amused; the screen's cold blue glow rakes the frame-RIGHT side of her face and the dark hall recedes behind her.

[3] SCREEN · INSERT · (SETUP D) — the feed fills the whole panel: the grid of stacked bubble-pods, and picked out nearer the centre one lit pod holding NYLA standing with one palm raised flat against the curve of her pod, tiny in the vast grid. No hall furniture and no elites in frame.

[4] HALL · MCU OTS · (SETUP C) — over OLD MAN's frame-RIGHT shoulder: his dark shoulder and white hair soft in the frame-RIGHT foreground corner, GOLD WOMAN's face sharp in the middle third at frame-LEFT, three-quarter to the lens, smiling as she finishes speaking, her glass still raised at the frame edge.

[5] HALL · MS TWO-SHOT · (SETUP H) — the room laughing softly, and the shot that establishes the pair: GOLD WOMAN in the frame-LEFT half and OLD MAN just beyond her in the frame-RIGHT half of the SAME frame, seated side by side with only the gold side table between them and nobody between them, shoulders almost touching, both at the same size and both in focus. Both are still seated squarely facing the screen, chairs and knees pointed exactly as in panel 1; only their heads and shoulders turn toward one another — she glances toward him mid-laugh with her glass raised, he tilts his head toward her with a faint smile, neither body rotated out of the row. The three other elites sit soft and small further down the row beyond him at frame-RIGHT, all of them also still facing the screen; the screen is not visible in this panel — it is behind camera, so its cool blue light falls on their faces from the front while the hall's arched alcoves, warm sconces and chandelier sit behind them.

[6] HALL · MCU OTS · (SETUP E) — the matched reverse of panel 4 at the same size: over GOLD WOMAN's frame-LEFT shoulder, her gold shoulder and swept-up hair soft in the frame-LEFT foreground corner, OLD MAN's face sharp in the middle third at frame-RIGHT, three-quarter to the lens, smiling faintly as he speaks.

[7] HALL · MCU · (SETUP F) — OLD MAN alone fills the middle third at frame-RIGHT, three-quarter to the lens, eyes frame-LEFT on the screen, the smile settling into something colder and more comfortable; blue screen-light rakes the frame-LEFT side of his face, warm sconce light behind him at frame-RIGHT.

[8] SCREEN · INSERT · (SETUP D) — the feed fills the whole panel again, pulled wider than panel 3 so hundreds of lit pods stretch to every edge, each holding its own pale sleeping figure, Nyla now only one dark standing speck among the many and no longer picked out; along the very bottom edge of the panel the silhouetted heads and shoulders of ALL FIVE elites sit small and dark against the glow — five distinct silhouettes, count them, never four — the cast, and the audience, in one frame.

[9] HALL · NEUTRAL WS · (SETUP G) — from the screen's own position looking back into the hall: the curved row of elites small and centred, every face lit cold blue from behind camera, glasses in hand, mid-laugh; the chandelier medallion and the arched alcoves recede into darkness above and behind them. The hall is watching us; we are standing where the pods are.

CONSTRAINTS: one action per panel; never mirror or flip a panel; GOLD WOMAN stays frame-LEFT of OLD MAN in every panel except the panel marked NEUTRAL, and the two are always seated adjacent with nobody between them; the sleepers in the feed are always pale and always lying down, one per pod, with the pods behind the front layer dark and unreadable; the hall always contains EXACTLY FIVE elites in every panel where the group is visible, never one more and never one fewer; a setup that places the camera between the row and the screen never shows the screen behind them, only its light on their faces; the two over-the-shoulder panels (4 and 6) are the same shot size over opposite shoulders, and panel 5's two-shot must agree with them by showing the pair seated adjacent; every elite in every HALL panel stays seated facing the screen and no chair is ever re-oriented — attention turns with heads and shoulders only; no character from the underground ever appears physically in the hall, only as picture on the screen; the screen is on and is the brightest source in every panel that shows it; state nothing on the screen that this prompt has not specified; keep every panel free of readable text except its number label.
