# BELOW THE FORECAST

## Story lock

**Logline:** On the exterior storm skin of Arcology Seven, climate technician Nia Vale discovers that the lethal weather she and other workers must survive is manufactured to power the luxury city watching from inside. During the season's biggest spectacle, she turns the forecast inward.

**Story law:** The privileged live in warm amber interiors behind sealed glass. Climate workers remain exposed to cyan storm light on the tower exterior. Every scene narrows the separation until Nia reverses it.

**Nia Vale:** Black woman, early 30s, lean industrial climber, close-cropped natural hair, weathered charcoal pressure suit with oxidized-copper harness, clear half visor, conductor hook clipped at her right hip. Precise, contained, observant; never glamorized or replaced.

**Lio Arden:** Nineteen-year-old sheltered heir, dark wavy hair, cream formal jacket, safe inside the tower. Curious rather than cruel. He is the first spectator to recognize Nia as a person.

**Arcology Seven:** Monumental brutalist luxury tower. Warm amber salons and polished stone inside; curved storm glass and exposed steel maintenance ribs outside. The lower city is dense, wet, and shadowed beneath the tower.

**Visual law:** cinematic photoreal live action, 16:9, grounded production design, anamorphic restraint, tactile rain and glass, amber-versus-cyan palette, believable scale, no illustration, no game UI, no copied glass tubes, ice climbing, bears, numbered prisoners, or reference compositions.

At breakfast, wealthy residents sit in a serene amber salon while, on the opposite side of its curved storm glass, Nia and a line of harnessed technicians crawl across the tower exterior in violent wind. One world treats the storm as ambience; the other must physically hold the building together.

Lio traces its route to the tower's central inversion wheel, then places his palm against the warm glass. Nia places her gloved palm opposite his—until an attendant steps between him and the window and closes a privacy screen.

### Scene 4 — The Engine Behind the Glass

The collapsing rib tears Nia through a maintenance hatch, but her harness catches her above the weather engine. She sees the gala floor physically pumping piston rods into the turbine whenever the crowd stamps and applauds. Lio watches from a protected service alcove adjoining the gallery and recognizes the same brass route model; he points Nia toward the mechanical inversion wheel.

**Storyboard prompt:** Cinematic photoreal 16:9 interior reveal inside Arcology Seven's hidden weather-engine chamber immediately after the exterior rib collapses. Nia Vale hangs caught by her oxidized-copper harness above the turbine, soaked and exhausted, face visible beneath a clear half visor, close-cropped natural hair, charcoal pressure suit, conductor hook still clipped at her right hip. Above her, the glass-bottomed gala floor flexes under stamping spectators; thick piston rods visibly descend from their feet and drive the turbine, which converts cyan lightning into warm amber conduits feeding the luxury floors. At one side, Lio Arden—the nineteen-year-old sheltered heir with dark wavy hair and cream formal jacket—remains safely enclosed in a dry stone service alcove adjoining the public weather gallery; beside him, the same tactile brass routing model continues into the chamber, and he points along it toward a large copper inversion wheel shaped to accept Nia's conductor hook. ### Scene 5 — The Forecast Falls Up

Nia locks her conductor hook into the inversion wheel and turns it. After the surge, the luxury atrium stands flooded and powerless while the exterior maintenance deck is calm for the first time. Workers enter through opened storm doors. Lio emerges from a protected stone stair after the danger has passed; he and Nia face each other with no glass between them.

**Storyboard prompt:** Cinematic photoreal 16:9 aftermath wide frame in Arcology Seven's luxury atrium moments after the forecast reversal, with no active storm and no flying debris. The powerless amber salon is flooded ankle-deep, chandeliers dark and dripping, wealthy gala guests huddled in stunned silence. Intact storm doors now stand open onto a completely calm exterior maintenance deck under clean cyan dawn. Nia Vale stands safely in the threshold, face visible beneath her clear half visor, close-cropped natural hair, charcoal pressure suit, oxidized-copper harness, conductor hook at her right hip, leading soaked climate workers inside. Lio Arden, nineteen-year-old sheltered heir, dry and unharmed in his cream formal jacket, emerges from a protected stone stairwell after the danger has passed and meets Nia's eyes across open air with no glass between them. Emotional reversal, class boundary physically opened rather than explosively shattered, hopeful but unresolved, grounded cinematic live action. No broken glass, tubes, ice climbing, bears, numbered prisoners, copied compositions, or readable text.
