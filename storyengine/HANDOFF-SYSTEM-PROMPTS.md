## What each prompt must encode (distill from POCOAPOCO-RECIPE.md)

**script** - the couple format, verbatim rules:
- Series: Ryan y Vanessa. Ryan attempts an everyday task in Spanish; Vanessa
  (native, patient) teaches. Comedy-warm, A1-A2, Spanish/English ~50/50 with
  every Spanish phrase paired with its English meaning IN THE SPOKEN LINES.
- Echo-guess-confirm-use loop on every vocab word; 5-6 words per episode,
  each drilled 3+ times; Ryan immediately USES each word in Spanglish.
- One planted small lie that pays off later + one physical disaster whose
  fix teaches a phrase; "poco a poco" name-drop worked in naturally.
- Quiz recap with the actual props and callback jokes; next-episode hook
  with one new word + a button joke paying off this episode's gag.
- Short lines, one speaker turn at a time; quick exchanges may be chained
  two-speaker turns. NO stage directions in spoken lines (EP2 was scrubbed
  of them - see HANDOFF.md).

**thumbnail** - the house style from thumbnail_new.png:
- Both characters (locked cast), the episode's DISASTER as the visual hook,
  stacked two-line title text: line 1 white, line 2 golden yellow, both with
  thick dark-navy outline + drop shadow; bright, high contrast; text large,
  minimal, correctly spelled.

**video_motion** - one concrete action per shot, no "gentle/soft/subtle"
(screensaver words), characters speak their exact scripted lines, no new
people, off-screen speakers never summoned. (Mirrors clip_dialogue guards -
mostly already enforced in code; the prompt should not fight them.)

**research / sound_*** - light touch: keep defaults unless something
contradicts the format (episodes are scripted comedy, not research-driven;
sound design is mostly grok-native ambience + the assembler).
