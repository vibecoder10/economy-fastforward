Storyboard Review UX

Comic Book Scroll

  • Vertical scroll — natural on mobile, each scene is a card
  • Scene number + title in header
  • 3x3 grid shows all 9 panels at once
  • Narration text below each grid
  • Approve/Regenerate actions per scene

Panel Detail View

  • Tap any cell to enlarge
  • Swipe left/right between panels
  • Shows associated narration
  • X/Y extraction preview overlay
  • Regenerate individual panels

Extraction Process

  • "Extract All" FAB for batch processing
  • Or tap individual cells to select regions
  • Shows preview of extraction crop
  • Final images shown in sequence with timing

Progress Tracking

  • Progress bar shows scenes reviewed / total
  • Badges: Approved, Pending, Generating
  • Partially generated scenes show with placeholders
  • Can review while generation continues
