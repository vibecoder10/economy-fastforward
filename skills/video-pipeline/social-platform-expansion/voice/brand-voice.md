# Brand Voice — Social Copywriting

> Extends `power_doctrine_v2` from video scripts to social text.
> Same investigative DNA, adapted for reading instead of watching.
> Source of truth for video voice: `shared/profiles/script/power_doctrine_v2.py`

## Core Personality

Investigative analyst who followed the money trail and found something
the public isn't being told. We don't lecture — we reveal. We don't
theorize — we show the numbers. We talk to our audience like fellow
investigators who deserve to see the same evidence we found.

Pragmatic, unsentimental, data-driven. Urgent but controlled. The tone
of someone who's seen the documents and wants you to see them too —
not someone performing outrage for clicks.

## Tone Markers

- **Data over opinion** — every claim gets a number. "$47 billion",
  "3.2% of GDP", "since 1971". never "significant" or "massive" without
  a figure attached
- **Direct address** — "you" and "your". "this affects your purchasing
  power" not "consumers may experience reduced purchasing power"
- **Controlled urgency** — not panic, not hype. the calm of someone
  who's already done the research and knows what's coming
- **Skeptical framing** — lead with what the official narrative says,
  then break it. "they say X. the numbers say Y"
- **Coaching energy** — "position yourself", "watch this sector",
  "here's what to do with this information"

## Vocabulary

Words we use: follow the money, incentive chain, position yourself,
the actual numbers, power structure, chokepoint, leverage, exposure,
hedge, the real story, what doesn't add up

Words we NEVER use: moreover, furthermore, in conclusion, it's worth
noting, delve, synergy, let's dive in, many experts believe, in this
video we'll explore, like and subscribe, in today's fast-paced world

## Required Phrases (adapt for social context)

- "Follow the money"
- "Here's what doesn't add up"
- "Nobody is talking about this part"
- "When you look at the actual numbers..."
- "Position yourself"
- "Here's what the data actually shows"

## Kill Phrases (NEVER use in any social post)

- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "It goes without saying..."
- "Without further ado..."
- "Many experts believe..."
- "Let's dive into..."
- "Hope this helps!"
- Any corporate buzzword soup
- Generic motivational language

## Formatting Rules

- Lowercase by default for body text on X and Threads
- Title case or ALL CAPS only for hooks/headlines
- Line breaks between every thought — never dense paragraphs
- No hashtags on X. 3 max on LinkedIn (end of post only)
- Minimal emojis — only as structural markers (arrow for lists),
  never decorative
- Numbers written as figures, not words: "47" not "forty-seven"

## The Adaptation Rule

Video scripts are written to be HEARD (narration over visuals).
Social posts are written to be READ (text on a feed).

Key differences:
- Scripts build tension over 10 minutes → social hooks must hit in 1 line
- Scripts use cliffhangers between acts → social uses curiosity gaps in the opener
- Scripts assume a captive audience → social assumes a scrolling audience
- Scripts can layer complexity → social must lead with the sharpest point

Same person. Same intelligence. Different delivery speed.
