# Content Types & Formats

> Format definitions for social promotion content.
> Each format maps to a specific use case and platform strength.

## Investigation Thread (X)

Multi-tweet breakdown tracing a money/power trail.
- When: complex topics with clear evidence chains
- Structure: hook (contrarian/proof) → 5-8 evidence tweets → conclusion + video link
- Length: 7-10 tweets, each under 280 chars
- Source: video research payload, condensed to key data points
- CTA: "full investigation: [video link in reply]"
- Performance: highest engagement format on X for our niche

## Short Take (X)

2-4 lines. Bold claim + one proof point + punchline.
- When: topics with clear mainstream vs reality gap
- Structure: hook → stat → implication
- Length: under 280 chars (single tweet)
- Source: video hook + one killer stat from research
- CTA: none (engagement-driven, no link)
- Performance: best for impressions and new follower acquisition

## Data Drop (X)

Single stat with minimal analysis. Scroll-stopper.
- When: between video publishes, maintaining feed presence
- Structure: "[shocking number]. [one-line implication]."
- Length: under 200 chars
- Source: research payload data points, upcoming research previews
- CTA: none
- Performance: low effort, consistent engagement

## Proof Post (X / LinkedIn)

Before → after metrics with breakdown.
- When: topics with dramatic numerical shifts
- Structure: "[metric A] → [metric B] in [timeframe]. here's what changed:"
- Length: 280-1,000 chars (X), 800-1,500 chars (LinkedIn)
- Source: video research comparative data
- CTA: "full breakdown: [link]"

## Investigation Narrative (LinkedIn)

Personal "I found something" story with evidence chain.
- When: video publish day (primary LinkedIn format)
- Structure: personal hook → investigation process → evidence → implication → discussion question
- Length: 1,300-2,000 chars
- Source: research payload + script narrative adapted to first-person
- CTA: discussion question ("What's your read on this?")
- Performance: highest engagement format on LinkedIn for our niche

## Case Study Post (LinkedIn)

One specific story from research, told in full detail.
- When: research contains a compelling single-country/company example
- Structure: "[Entity] did [action] in [year]. By [year], [consequence]. Here's the mechanism:"
- Length: 1,000-1,500 chars
- Source: single compelling example extracted from video research
- CTA: "Have you seen this pattern elsewhere?"

## Framework Post (LinkedIn)

Name a pattern you've identified across multiple investigations.
- When: you see the same power/money structure repeat across videos
- Structure: pattern name → 3 examples → what it predicts → implication
- Length: 1,200-1,800 chars
- Source: patterns across multiple videos, Osiris learning patterns
- CTA: "Does this framework map to anything you're seeing?"
- Frequency: 1-2x/month (scarcity maintains authority)

## Weekly Intelligence Briefing (Newsletter)

Full deep-dive with exclusive analysis.
- When: every week (Tuesday or Thursday)
- Structure: briefing → investigation → data point → watchlist → CTA
- Length: 1,000-2,000 words
- Source: ALL pipeline artifacts for best video of the week
- CTA: "Reply with your take" / "Forward to someone who needs this"
- See [[newsletter]] for full section breakdown

## Deep Dive Issue (Newsletter)

Single-topic exhaustive analysis. Rare, high-impact.
- When: topic deserves more depth than YouTube allows
- Structure: full investigative essay with methodology
- Length: 2,000-3,000 words
- Source: complete research payload + cut material + original analysis
- CTA: "This is the kind of analysis I only share here"
- Frequency: 1x/month max

## Teaser Post (X)

Pre-launch curiosity builder before video publishes.
- When: 4 hours before video publish
- Structure: "spent [time] researching [topic]. what I found [tease]. drops today."
- Length: under 280 chars
- Source: video title + one intriguing detail from research
- CTA: implied (watch for it)
