# Scheduling & Posting Calendar

> Timed around the video pipeline's publish schedule.
> Videos publish via `upload/` bot, usually scheduled for
> optimal YouTube algorithm windows. Social promotion
> wraps around that publish time.

## Weekly Frequency

| Platform | Posts/Week | Type |
|----------|-----------|------|
| X | 5-7 | 1-2 video promos + 3-5 standalone data drops/takes |
| LinkedIn | 3 | 1-2 video promos + 1-2 standalone analysis posts |
| Newsletter | 1 | Weekly digest with deep-dive on best video topic |

## Video Publish Day Schedule

Relative to video publish time (T):

| Time | Platform | Post Type |
|------|----------|-----------|
| T-4h | X | Teaser: "spent [time] researching [topic]. video drops today" |
| T+0 | YouTube | Video publishes |
| T+1h | X | Main promotion post (thread or proof post with link in reply) |
| T+2h | LinkedIn | Investigation narrative post (link in first comment) |
| T+30min | X | Engage in replies to main post (algo boost window) |
| T+24h | X | Follow-up data point from the same research |

## Non-Publish Days

Fill the content calendar between videos:

| Day Type | X Content | LinkedIn Content |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Research day | Data drop: one stat from upcoming research | — |
| Analysis day | Short take: contrarian angle on current news | Framework or case study post |
| Engagement day | Quote-tweet with analysis on trending topic | Comment engagement (10+ posts) |

## Newsletter Schedule

- Send: Tuesday or Thursday, 8-10am reader timezone
- Content: deep-dive from week's best video + data points + watchlist
- NEVER skip a week. consistency builds inbox trust
- If no video that week: "what I'm researching" issue

## Peak Posting Times

| Platform | Best Windows (US timezones) |
|----------|---------------------------|
| X | 8-9am ET, 12-1pm ET, 5-6pm ET |
| LinkedIn | 7-8am ET, 12pm ET, 5-6pm ET |
| Newsletter | 8-10am reader's timezone |

## Batch Workflow

### Sunday/Monday Planning Session
1. Check video pipeline: what's publishing this week?
2. Pull research payloads for each upcoming video
3. Pre-write all X teasers and main promotion posts
4. Pre-write LinkedIn investigation narratives
5. Draft newsletter deep-dive section
6. Schedule everything (Buffer, Typefully, or native schedulers)
7. Queue 3-5 standalone data drops for non-publish days

### Daily (15 min)
1. Check engagement on published posts
2. Reply to comments in the first hour after posting
3. Note which hooks drove YouTube clicks (for Osiris feedback)

### Friday Review
1. Which posts drove the most YouTube views? → feed to Osiris
2. Which hook types got best engagement? → update [[hooks]]
3. Best performing platform this week? → adjust next week's focus
4. Subscriber growth? → newsletter health check
