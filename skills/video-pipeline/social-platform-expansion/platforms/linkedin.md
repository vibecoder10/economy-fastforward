# LinkedIn — Platform Playbook

## Platform DNA

- No hard character limit, sweet spot 1,300-2,000 characters
- Vibe: professional but human. personal investigation narratives
  with geopolitical insight. "I" stories with data attached
- Audience: mix of [[geopolitics-enthusiasts]] and [[casual-observers]]
  — more professionals, finance people, policy watchers, people
  with actual decision-making power

## Why LinkedIn Matters for Us

LinkedIn is the PROFESSIONAL REACH channel. The audience here has
higher purchasing power, more influence, and is more likely to
share content with their professional network. A LinkedIn post
that resonates gets forwarded in corporate Slack channels and
boardroom conversations.

The goal: position as the investigative source that professionals
trust for geopolitical intelligence.

## Content Rules

- Lead with a personal investigation hook. "I spent 3 weeks tracking..."
  / "I was wrong about..." / "Everyone told me this was about X.
  The numbers say it's about Y"
- First line is EVERYTHING. LinkedIn truncates after ~210 characters
  with a "see more" button. the hook MUST be in that first line
- Use [[hooks]] — proof hooks and behind-the-scenes hooks perform best
- Match [[brand-voice]] but more authoritative. see [[platform-tone]]
- Short paragraphs. one insight per paragraph. white space matters
- No hashtags in the body. max 3 at the very end if any
- Links in the first comment, never in the post body. algo penalizes
- Include your analytical process: "When I mapped the fund flows..."
  "The third data point changed everything..."

## Formats That Work

### 1. The Investigation Narrative
"I [tracked/researched/discovered something] → here's what I found →
here's what it means for [your industry/portfolio/understanding]"
- Source: video's research payload + personal framing
- LinkedIn's bread and butter. the "I followed the money" story
- 1,500-2,000 chars. full narrative arc

### 2. The Contrarian Analysis
Challenge conventional wisdom with specific data. open with bold
claim, follow with evidence chain, close with implications.
- Source: video's "lead with the lie" (Act 1) expanded
- "Everyone says [mainstream take]. But when you look at [specific
  data], the picture is completely different"
- 1,000-1,500 chars

### 3. The Data Breakdown
"[N] things the [topic] data actually shows:" with numbered items.
Each item: one stat + one-sentence implication.
- Source: research payload distilled to 5-7 key findings
- Good for complex topics that need structure
- 1,300-1,800 chars

### 4. The Framework Post
Name a pattern you see across multiple investigations.
"I keep finding the same structure in [topic area]. I call it
[framework name]. Here's how it works:"
- Source: patterns across multiple videos / Osiris learnings
- Establishes intellectual authority
- Use sparingly: 1-2x/month

### 5. The Case Study
One specific story from the research, told in full. Real country,
real numbers, real timeline, real consequences.
- Source: single compelling example from video research
- "In 2019, Country X did [action]. By 2024, [consequence].
  The mechanism was [explanation]"
- Most shareable format on LinkedIn

## Posting Strategy

- Frequency: 3-5x/week
- Video publish day: investigation narrative post (AM)
- Best times: 7-8am, 12pm, 5-6pm (business hours, US/EU)
- Comment on 10 relevant geopolitics/finance posts before publishing
  yours (engagement seed)
- See [[scheduling]] for full calendar

## Audience on LinkedIn

- Mix of [[geopolitics-enthusiasts]] and [[casual-observers]]
- Skews professional: finance, policy, consulting, defense, energy
- They want: analysis they can cite in meetings, data they can
  reference in reports, frameworks for understanding complex events
- They don't want: partisan framing, clickbait without substance,
  overly casual language that undermines credibility
- Higher-value audience than X — these people make decisions

## Repurposing Notes

- LinkedIn is the SECOND platform in the [[repurpose]] chain
- Take the X post → add investigation narrative, expand to 1,300+ chars
- Add professional framing ("In tracking global fund flows...")
- The hook almost always needs a full rewrite — X's casual punch
  doesn't translate to LinkedIn's authority tone
- Key stat from X becomes supporting evidence in LinkedIn's story
- See [[repurpose]] for full chain

## Pipeline Data Sources

When generating LinkedIn content for a video:
```
Research payload → investigation story, evidence chain, sources
Script Acts 1-2  → narrative setup (adapted from spoken to written)
Video hook       → rewritten as personal investigation opener
SEO summary      → core topic framing
Curiosity gap    → which cognitive pattern anchors the post
Channel profile  → emotional arc template (setup → build → turn → payoff)
```
