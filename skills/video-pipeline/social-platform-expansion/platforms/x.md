# X / Twitter — Platform Playbook

## Platform DNA

- Character limit: 280 per tweet, long-form up to 25,000
  (sweet spot: 800-2000 characters for long-form)
- Vibe: fast, opinion-driven, proof-driven. lowercase default.
  you have 0.5 seconds to stop a scroll
- Audience here: more analytical, more [[geopolitics-enthusiasts]]
  than [[casual-observers]]. they want the data, not the lecture

## Why X Matters for Us

X is the DISCOVERY ENGINE. This is where new viewers find us.
Geopolitics Twitter is massive and hungry for data-backed takes.
Every viral post is a funnel to the YouTube video.

The goal of every X post: make someone curious enough to watch
the full investigation on YouTube.

## Content Rules

- Write this platform FIRST in the [[repurpose]] chain. X forces
  you to find the single sharpest point from the video's research
- Use [[hooks]] — contrarian hooks and proof hooks perform best.
  Cross-reference with Osiris CTR data for the video's curiosity
  gap structure
- Match [[brand-voice]] at its most casual. see [[platform-tone]]
- Line breaks between every thought. never dense paragraphs
- No hashtags. ever. they look desperate on X
- Links go in a REPLY to your own post, never in the main tweet.
  algo penalizes external links in main tweet
- Numbers always as figures: "$47B" not "forty-seven billion"

## Formats That Work

### 1. The Investigation Thread
"[Shocking stat or claim]. here's what nobody is connecting:"
followed by 5-8 tweets tracing the money/power trail.
- Source: video's research payload, condensed to key data points
- End thread with: "full investigation: [link in reply]"
- Best for: complex topics with clear evidence chains

### 2. The Short Take (Contrarian)
2-4 lines. bold contrarian claim + one-line proof + punchline.
- Source: video's hook + one killer stat from research
- "they say the economy is strong. consumer debt just hit $17.3T.
  follow the money."
- Best for: topics with clear mainstream vs reality gap

### 3. The Proof Post
"[Before metric] → [after metric] in [timeframe]"
followed by the 3-sentence breakdown.
- Source: video's SEO keywords + core data from research
- "$0.35 → $6.80 per gallon in Venezuela. same oil reserves.
  here's what changed (it wasn't supply):"
- Best for: topics with dramatic numerical shifts

### 4. The Teaser
Pre-launch post that builds curiosity before video publishes.
- "spent 3 weeks researching [topic]. what I found changes
  everything about how you should think about [related thing].
  video drops tomorrow."
- Best for: high-conviction videos where research was deep

### 5. The Data Drop
Single stat + personal analysis in under 280 chars.
- "$2.1 trillion left china in 18 months. nobody is asking where it went."
- Best for: quick engagement between video publishes

## Posting Strategy

- Frequency: 5-7x/week (1-2 posts per day)
- Video publish day: teaser AM + link post PM
- Non-publish days: data drops, short takes, engagement replies
- Best times: 8-9am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm (US timezones — our audience)
- Engage in replies for 30 min after posting (algo boost)
- See [[scheduling]] for full calendar

## Audience on X

- Primary: [[geopolitics-enthusiasts]] — people who already follow
  geopolitics accounts, finance Twitter, macro economics
- They want: specific numbers, source citations, contrarian angles
  backed by data, actionable insight ("position yourself")
- They don't want: vague hot takes, partisan framing, "experts say"
  without showing which experts and what data
- Address them as "you" — peer energy, fellow investigators

## Pipeline Data Sources

When generating X content for a video:
```
Research payload → key stats, data points, source citations
Script Act 1     → "lead with the lie" (official narrative to break)
Video hook       → adapt from spoken hook to written hook
SEO keywords     → topic framing and discoverability
Title structure  → curiosity gap pattern (HIDDEN_FLAW, TIME_BOMB, etc.)
Osiris learnings → which hook types got best CTR historically
```
