# Audience: Geopolitics Enthusiasts (Primary)

> Maps to `power_doctrine_v2` audience definition:
> "Ambitious 18-45 adults who want to understand how power
> and money actually move. Skeptical of mainstream narratives."

## Who They Are

People who already follow geopolitics, macro economics, and
global power dynamics. They read Foreign Affairs, follow finance
Twitter, watch documentary channels, and track commodity prices.

Demographics: 18-45, predominantly male (75%+), global but
US/EU/MENA heavy. Mix of professionals (finance, policy, defense,
energy, consulting) and self-educated enthusiasts.

They don't trust mainstream news framing. They've been burned by
narratives that turned out to be wrong. They want someone who
shows the data and lets them decide.

## What They Want

- **Specific numbers** — "$47B", "3.2% of GDP", "since 1971".
  never vague claims. they check your numbers
- **Follow the money** analysis — who benefits, who pays, where
  the money actually went. incentive chains, not opinions
- **Contrarian angles backed by data** — not contrarian for shock
  value, contrarian because the data contradicts the mainstream take
- **Actionable implications** — "position yourself", "watch this
  sector", "here's what this means for your portfolio/understanding"
- **Primary sources** — they respect citations. link the IMF report,
  reference the earnings call, cite the trade data

## What They Don't Want

- Vague hot takes without data ("the economy is about to crash!")
- Partisan framing (left/right lens instead of follow-the-money lens)
- "Many experts believe..." without naming which experts
- Mainstream narrative repackaged as analysis
- Lectures and condescension
- Generic motivational content

## How to Talk to Them

- Direct and specific. "the data shows" not "it seems like"
- Peer energy — fellow investigators sharing findings
- Respect their intelligence. they can handle complexity
- Challenge them: "if you're not watching [this indicator],
  you're missing the real story"
- Use the vocabulary they already use: hedge, exposure, chokepoint,
  leverage, positioning, supply chain, reserve currency
- Address as "you" — personal, direct, coaching

## Platform Behavior

- **X:** they're in the replies, quoting with their own analysis,
  building threads on top of your data. they engage deeply
- **LinkedIn:** they share with professional context, "relevant
  to what we're seeing in [their industry]"
- **Newsletter:** they reply with questions, counterarguments,
  and their own data points. highest-value interaction
