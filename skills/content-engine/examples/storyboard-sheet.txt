BEATS + COVERAGE EXAMPLE (worked, proven on the fishing demo)

A "beat" is one moment shown from several matched angles. First shot = master
(fully establishes the moment); the rest say "same moment, camera now [angle]" and
get generated off the master so lighting/setting match. Keep screen direction
consistent within a beat. Inserts (a prop, a detail) bridge cuts.

Pacing guide for `cut`: wide/establishing 3-4s, medium 2-2.5s, close 2s, insert
1.4-1.8s. Quicker cuts during action, hold the establishing and closing longer.

dialogue: set it on shots where the character speaks. grok lip-syncs it. The voice
is random per clip, so use it for one-off lines, not a consistent recurring voice.
